## Operations

<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion id="who-pages-whom" title="Something broke. Who do we page?">
1. Capture evidence per [Troubleshooting → Before you debug](/docs/troubleshooting#before-you-debug).
2. Check [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting) and the [FAQ](/docs/faq).
3. Page the on-call owner from [Handoff → Ownership](/docs/handoff#ownership).
4. If still stuck, contact __SE_NAME__ with the evidence.
</Accordion>

<Accordion id="sandbox-refresh" title="We refreshed the sandbox — what breaks?">
Named Credentials and assigned permsets are wiped during a full-copy refresh.
Re-authenticate Named Credentials and re-run the permset assignment commands from
[Setup → Install](/docs/setup#install). See [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting)
for the full list.
</Accordion>

<Accordion id="how-monitor" title="How do we know it's healthy?">
See [Observability → SLO snapshot](/docs/observability#slo-snapshot) and the
review cadence on the same page. The short answer: weekly Apex log scan,
weekly transcript spot-check, monthly permset audit.
</Accordion>

</Accordions>
