<Callout type="info" title="Three phases, three exit criteria">
Each phase has explicit go/no-go criteria. Don't move forward until the criteria are
green — it's cheaper to delay a phase than to roll back from production.
</Callout>

## Phases

### Phase 1 — Pilot

- **Audience**: 5–10 hand-picked users from __PERSONAS__
- **Duration**: 2 weeks
- **Goal**: validate happy path with real (non-test) data; collect qualitative feedback

**Exit criteria:**

- [ ] ≥ 80% of pilot users complete the happy-path scenario unaided
- [ ] Zero P1 incidents
- [ ] Pilot retro completed; top 3 issues either fixed or scoped out

### Phase 2 — Expand

- **Audience**: One full team / business unit (~50 users)
- **Duration**: 4 weeks
- **Goal**: validate at scale; tune thresholds; train tier-1 support

**Exit criteria:**

- [ ] SLOs from [Observability](/docs/observability#slo-snapshot) met for the full window
- [ ] Tier-1 support team trained and resolving ≥ 70% of tickets without escalation
- [ ] Customer-side change-management approval for general availability

### Phase 3 — General availability

- **Audience**: All __PERSONAS__
- **Duration**: ongoing
- **Goal**: production operations, continuous improvement

## Rollback plan

<Callout type="warn" title="Define rollback before you need it">
The day you need a rollback is the day you find out you don't have one. Test the
rollback in a sandbox before going to GA.
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Disable** the entry point — deactivate the Agentforce agent or unschedule the
    triggering flow. Users see the prior behavior immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Communicate** to the pilot group within 1 hour using the comms channels above.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Investigate** with evidence captured per [Troubleshooting → Before you debug](/docs/troubleshooting#before-you-debug).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Decide**: roll forward with a fix, stay rolled back, or escalate to __SE_NAME__.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Open decisions

- [ ] Pilot user list signed off by sponsor
- [ ] Pilot exit criteria signed off by sponsor
- [ ] GA date target (with realistic buffer)
- [ ] Owner for the post-launch hyper-care window (typically 2 weeks)
